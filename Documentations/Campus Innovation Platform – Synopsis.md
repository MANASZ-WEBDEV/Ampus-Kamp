# Campus Innovation Platform

### An event-discovery, team-formation and innovation-analytics system for colleges

*Based on Smart India Hackathon problem statement SIH1643 – Innovation Excellence Analytics Portal*

---

## 1. Abstract

Colleges produce a large amount of innovation activity every year: hackathons, competitions, sports, workshops, research papers, patents, grants and awards. This information is scattered across WhatsApp messages, emails and spreadsheets. Students miss opportunities because event details arrive as long, unstructured messages, and solo students struggle to find teammates. Administrators, in turn, cannot easily answer questions such as "which department is performing best in innovation?".

This project proposes a single web platform (Progressive Web App) that (1) converts faculty announcements into structured event cards and notifies students, (2) lets students find and join teams, (3) records and verifies participation and achievements, and (4) automatically turns that verified data into department-wise dashboards, rankings and reports, as required by SIH1643. An AI-based extraction module reads raw announcement text and fills in event fields, which also forms the research component of the project.

## 2. Problem Statement

1. **Information overload:** faculty send long messages for every hackathon or contest; important details (deadline, eligibility, team size, link) get lost.
2. **Team formation difficulty:** solo students do not know who else is interested or what skills are available.
3. **Scattered records:** achievements are tracked manually in separate files, so reports take time and are error-prone.
4. **No unified innovation view:** the institution has no single, verified picture of its innovation performance (SIH1643 asks for patents, papers, grants, startups, competitions, awards, department-wise dashboards, rankings and reports).

## 3. Objectives

- Provide one place where all events, contests and sports announcements are listed in a structured, searchable form.
- Reduce faculty effort by converting pasted announcements into structured cards automatically.
- Notify students on time through push notifications, email and optionally Telegram.
- Help solo students find teams based on branch, year and skills.
- Log participation and results with proof, and verify them through a faculty/admin workflow.
- Generate department-wise dashboards, rankings and reports automatically from verified data.
- Evaluate the accuracy of the AI extraction module and the stability of the innovation index (research objective).

## 4. Existing System and Its Limitations

| Current practice | Limitation |
| --- | --- |
| WhatsApp group announcements | Long messages, no filters, no reminders, buried by newer messages |
| Third-party platforms (hackathon/event portals) | Not specific to the college, no internal team finder, no institute analytics |
| Excel sheets for achievements | Manual entry, no verification, no real-time dashboards |
| Word-of-mouth team formation | Limited to a student's own circle |

## 5. Proposed System

The platform connects four stages in one flow:

```
Faculty pastes event message → system extracts a structured event card
→ students notified (push / email / Telegram)
→ solo students post "looking for teammates" → others request → owner accepts
→ team connects over WhatsApp
→ after the event: result/certificate uploaded → verified by faculty/admin
→ verified data feeds counts, rankings, trends and reports
```

**Key idea:** nobody creates groups or reports manually. Departments, event types and years are attributes of each record, and dashboards are generated by aggregating those records.

## 6. User Roles

| Role | Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| Student | Create profile, browse events, post/join team requests, upload results |
| Faculty / coordinator | Paste announcements, confirm extracted cards, verify results, add own achievements |
| Department | View department dashboard and rankings |
| Admin | Approve users, view institute-wide analytics, configure index weights, export reports |

## 7. Modules and Features

### 7.1 Authentication and Profiles

- Login using a valid enrollment number, checked against a pre-loaded student list, plus verification by email OTP (or admin approval where no college email exists).
- Profile: name, branch, year, skills, optional phone number visibility setting.

### 7.2 Event Feed

- Events of types: hackathon, competition, sports, workshop, seminar, other.
- Filters by type, deadline, branch/year eligibility; bookmarks; deadline reminders.

### 7.3 Paste-to-Post Extraction (AI module)

- Faculty paste the same long message they already send.
- The system extracts: title, type, organizer, deadline, event date, eligibility, team size, fee, link and a short summary.
- Faculty review and confirm the card before it is published.
- Raw messages and confirmed cards are stored as a labeled dataset for evaluation.

### 7.4 Team Finder

- A student posts a team request for an event, stating the roles and number of spots needed.
- Other students send join requests; the owner accepts or rejects.
- After acceptance, the WhatsApp link or number is shown, so the app does not need its own chat system.

### 7.5 Participation and Verification

- Teams or individuals record participation and upload proof (certificate, result screenshot).
- Status flow: Pending → Verified / Rejected, with the verifier and time recorded.

### 7.6 Analytics and Reports (SIH1643 core)

- Counts by department, event type and year.
- Department ranking with an explanation of how each indicator contributed.
- Innovation index with admin-configurable weights.
- Downloadable annual report (PDF).
- Faculty and institute achievements (papers, patents, grants, startups, awards) can be added through the same upload and verification workflow.

## 8. System Architecture

## 9. Technology Stack

| Layer | Technology |
| --- | --- |
| Frontend | React, PWA (service worker, web push) |
| Backend | Node.js, Express, JWT authentication |
| Database | MongoDB (flexible records, aggregation pipelines) |
| File storage | Cloud object storage (e.g., Cloudinary/S3-compatible) |
| AI extraction | LLM API returning strict JSON, validated server-side |
| Notifications | Web push, email, optional Telegram bot |
| Charts and reports | Chart library on the frontend, PDF generation on the server |

**Why a web app (PWA) instead of a native app:** one codebase for Android, iOS and laptops; no store installation barrier; instant updates; and faculty can use the desktop view for posting. A known limitation is that web push on iPhone requires adding the app to the home screen, so email and Telegram are provided as fallback channels.

## 10. Database Design (core collections)

```
User          { enrollmentNo, name, email, branch, year, role, skills[], phoneVisible }
Event         { title, type, organizer, deadline, eventDate, eligibility, teamSize,
                fee, link, summary, rawMessage, postedBy, status }
TeamPost      { eventId, ownerId, rolesNeeded[], spotsLeft, description, status }
JoinRequest   { teamPostId, userId, message, status }
Participation { eventId, userIds[], teamName, result, proofUrl,
                verification: { status, verifiedBy, verifiedAt } }
```

Dashboards are aggregations over `Participation` joined with `User.branch`, `Event.type` and date fields.

## 11. Methodology

### 11.1 Event extraction

1. Faculty submit raw text.
2. The backend sends it to the LLM with a fixed schema and instructions to return only JSON.
3. The response is validated (required fields, date formats, URL checks). Missing or uncertain fields are left empty for the faculty to fill.
4. The confirmed card is saved with the raw message.

### 11.2 Innovation index

Start with weighted counts of verified items (competitions, awards, patents, papers, grants, startups). Weights are stored as admin settings, not hard-coded. The report shows each indicator's contribution so rankings are explainable.

### 11.3 Verification

Every record carries a status. Only verified records enter the analytics, which prevents fake or unchecked entries from affecting rankings.

## 12. Research Component

**Primary research question:** how accurately can an LLM-based pipeline extract structured event details from unstructured college announcements, compared with rule-based extraction?

- **Dataset:** 150–300 real announcement messages collected from the college (with permission), labeled by hand.
- **Methods compared:** rule/regex baseline vs. LLM-based extraction.
- **Metrics:** field-level precision, recall and F1; fraction of cards confirmed without edits; time saved per post.

**Secondary question:** how stable are department rankings when the index weights change? Method: sensitivity analysis and rank-correlation across several weighting schemes.

**Optional:** whether skill-based teammate suggestions increase accepted join requests compared with a plain list, tested in a small pilot with one batch.

*Note:* long-term forecasting is deliberately excluded because a single college has too few yearly data points for reliable prediction.

## 13. Expected Outcomes

- A working platform deployed and piloted with real students.
- Faculty can publish events in seconds from existing messages.
- Verified, automatically generated department dashboards and annual reports.
- Measured extraction accuracy and a documented index-sensitivity analysis suitable for a conference paper.

## 15. Risks and Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Low adoption (empty app) | Seed real events; pilot with a batch; make posting easy for faculty |
| Wrong AI extraction | Faculty confirm every card; validate JSON; keep raw text |
| Fake achievements | Proof upload plus faculty/admin verification |
| Privacy of student data | College-only access, hidden phone numbers until a request is accepted, permission for any data used in the paper |
| Scope too large | Prioritize core flow and the extraction evaluation; cut analytics extras first |

## 16. Future Scope

- Automatic reading of certificates and papers (OCR plus extraction).
- Topic analysis of research output; collaboration graphs between faculty.
- Teammate recommendation using skills and past participation.
- Integration with college ERP and other institutions.
